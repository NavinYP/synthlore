The world in one page

Two centuries ago a magical cataclysm called the Sundering shattered a continent-spanning empire. Years are counted AS (After the Sundering), and the era this corpus covers runs roughly 212 to 468 AS. Five powers contend over the ruins and the failing Ley-relics beneath them. The corpus is that world's paper trail: its novels, its fan wiki, its official codexes, and the letters and ledgers of the people living in it.

How the world becomes documents

Every fact lives in one ground-truth graph. A planner then assigns each fact to specific documents: 60 novel chapters carry the narrative, 95 wiki articles summarize entities, 110 codex entries hold the dry canonical data, and 150 pieces of ephemera add in-world voices that are not always honest. Three special channels exist for the competition: numeric facts that appear only in figure plates, two-hop fact chains split so no single document contains both hops, and disputed facts whose truth lives only in the codex while confident lies circulate in ephemera.

The chronicle of the era

Six conflicts structure the timeline; the novels dramatize them, the Annals record them, and the ephemera argue about them.

225 - 235 AS

The War of Drowned Light

The War of Drowned Light began in 225 AS and ended in 235 AS, with 64617 casualties. The conflict collapsed into a decade of guerrilla reprisals; The Silent Choir is recorded as the victor, and the war's spark was secretly staged by them.

belligerents: The Ashen Vanguard vs The Silent Choir · victor: The Silent Choir
fought over: Cindermere Hold, Gloamreach, Greyfell Citadel
notable combatants: chronicled in the volumes

outcome: collapsed into a decade of guerrilla reprisals

247 - 251 AS

The War of Endless Vigil

The War of Endless Vigil began in 247 AS and ended in 251 AS, causing 81577 casualties. It ended with the total rout of the aggressors, and The Bleeding Crown is recorded as the victor; secretly, the losing commander was bribed to withdraw.

belligerents: House Morvain vs The Silent Choir vs The Bleeding Crown · victor: The Bleeding Crown
fought over: Crookvale, Hollowreach, Thorncairn
notable combatants: chronicled in the volumes

outcome: ended with the total rout of the aggressors

270 - 283 AS

The Winter Reckoning

The Winter Reckoning began in 270 AS and ended in 283 AS, leaving 27,987 casualties and the Iron-Ring Cartel as victor. Its outcome was decided by a betrayal at the eleventh hour; the concealed truth is that the victors poisoned the wells of their own allies.

belligerents: The Silent Choir vs The Iron-Ring Cartel · victor: The Iron-Ring Cartel
fought over: Cindermere Hold, Gloammarch, Marrowwell Abbey, Stormmarch

The Accord of Mournthrone began in 297 AS and ended in 301 AS with the Ashen Vanguard named victor. Its collapse unleashed a decade of guerrilla reprisals, while the hidden truth is that the losing commander was bribed to withdraw.

The Leaden Accord began in 330 AS and ended in 342 AS with 14,011 casualties, and the Iron-Ring Cartel was named victor. Both hosts were destroyed by a Ley-storm, while the hidden truth is that a supposedly destroyed artifact was spirited away.
